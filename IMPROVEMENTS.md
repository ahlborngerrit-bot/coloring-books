# Coloring Book Generator - Enhanced Line Art

## What's New

This generator has been significantly improved to produce **professional-quality coloring book pages** with clean, bold black-and-white line art.

### Key Improvements

1. **Enhanced Edge Detection** - Three sophisticated line art extraction methods:
   - **Enhanced** (recommended): Thick, bold lines perfect for easy coloring
   - **Standard**: Balanced approach for general use
   - **Detailed**: Fine lines for intricate designs

2. **Better AI Prompting** - Optimized prompts that generate images which convert beautifully to line art

3. **Multi-Stage Processing** - Advanced image processing pipeline:
   - Bilateral filtering to preserve edges
   - Adaptive thresholding for clean outlines
   - Canny edge detection for precision
   - Morphological operations for thick, coloring-friendly lines
   - Final cleanup for pure black-on-white

## Line Art Methods Explained

### Enhanced (Recommended)
- **Best for**: Adult coloring books, easy coloring
- **Characteristics**: Thick, bold outlines (2-3 pixels wide)
- **Technique**: Bilateral filter + adaptive threshold + dilated edges
- **Use case**: When you want clear, easy-to-color pages

### Standard
- **Best for**: General purpose coloring books
- **Characteristics**: Medium thickness (1-2 pixels)
- **Technique**: Gaussian blur + Canny edges + light dilation
- **Use case**: Balanced detail and ease of use

### Detailed
- **Best for**: Intricate, complex designs
- **Characteristics**: Fine, precise lines (1 pixel)
- **Technique**: Sensitive edge detection, minimal processing
- **Use case**: When you want maximum detail preservation

## Technical Details

### Image Processing Pipeline

When `--force-lineart` is enabled:

1. **Load Image**: Decode from AI-generated bytes
2. **Grayscale Conversion**: Convert to single channel
3. **Bilateral Filter**: Smooth while preserving edges
4. **Adaptive Threshold**: Find dark regions
5. **Canny Edge Detection**: Detect precise edges
6. **Combine & Dilate**: Merge edges, thicken lines
7. **Cleanup**: Remove noise, ensure pure B&W
8. **Threshold**: Force white background (250+ → 255)
9. **Export**: High-quality PNG at 300 DPI

### Why This Works Better

Previous approach:
- ❌ Asked AI to generate "line art" directly

New approach:
